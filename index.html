📱 How to study from your Tablet (iPad or Android)?

This application is optimized as a PWA (Progressive Web App) and works seamlessly offline:

  • On iPad (Safari): Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up) and select "Add to Home Screen". It opens as a full-screen standalone app.
  • On Android (Chrome): Tap the menu (three dots) and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  • Touch Gestures: Swipe your finger left or right across the screen to quickly move to the next or previous question.
  • PC ↔ Tablet Sync: Use GitHub Gist automatic sync or use "Export" and "Import" to transfer your progress manually.
